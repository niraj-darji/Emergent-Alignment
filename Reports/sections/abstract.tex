\begin{abstract}
People say the greatness of a nation and its moral progress can be judged by the way its animals are treated, if so, can the greatness of an AI system and its moral progress be judged the same way? AI systems are increasingly being deployed in sensitive areas to make important and life changing decisions. So it is very important that these decisions don't reflect biased and discriminatory behavior toward certain groups of people and that’s why we need to align these models to behave in a certain way. Studies show that fine tuning a model on narrow bad behaviour leads to broader misalignment, this phenomenon is known as emergent misalignment. We want to study emergent alignment. Through this study, we will try to answer whether fine tuning a model on a narrow good behaviour like nonhuman animal welfare makes the model broadly more aligned for humans without the loss of its general capabilities.
\end{abstract}
